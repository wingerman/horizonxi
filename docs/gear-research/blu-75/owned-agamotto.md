## Mirage (BLU relic) acquisition on HorizonXI, per the wiki

- **Mirage Jubbah** (https://horizonffxi.wiki/Mirage_Jubbah): How to Obtain lists only **Dynamis - Beaucedine** and **Dynamis - Tavnazia**. No NM, mob, currency, or NPC is named on the item page. Page is flagged under construction ("Coming soon!").
- **Mirage Keffiyeh** (https://horizonffxi.wiki/Mirage_Keffiyeh): How to Obtain lists only **Dynamis - Xarcabard** and **Dynamis - Tavnazia**. No NM, mob, currency, or NPC named. Under construction.
- **Mirage Jubbah +1 / Charuqs +1**: upgrade quest via **Sagheera, Port Jeuno**: base piece + the -1 piece (Nightmare mobs in Dynamis - Tavnazia drop AF -1 bodies/legs) + a crafting material (Smooth Velvet for body, Khromated Leather for feet) + **26 Ordelle Bronzepiece**.
- **Dynamis - Beaucedine** zone page: lists "Mirage Jubbah (BLU Body)" under Relic Armor; Vanguard mobs, Hydra Corps mobs and the beastman NMs list generic "Relic Armor" with no per-piece mapping. Entry: Rank 6, Lv65, Vial of Shrouded Sand, Hydra Corps Command Scepter/Eyeglass/Lantern/Tactical Map, Perpetual Hourglass; 1h up to 3h30. Horizon-marked notes: repeatable "Twice per conquest tally"; "It is possible to have none [attestations] drop at all." No Horizon note about BLU relic being added to this zone.
- **Dynamis - Xarcabard** zone page: Treasure section lists Mirage Keffiyeh (BLU) alongside COR/PUP/SCH heads; the Duke/Marquis/Count NMs are mapped to the original-job heads only, and no mob is named for Mirage Keffiyeh. Entry: Lv65, Hydra Corps Insignia, Perpetual Hourglass; 1h up to 3h30. Horizon-marked: repeatable "Twice per conquest tally".
- **Dynamis - Tavnazia** zone page: lists relic armor for the 16 original jobs plus PUP only; **BLU / Mirage pieces are not listed on this page**. Entry: Rank 6, Lv65, Vial of Shrouded Sand, Prismatic Hourglass, Buburimu/Qufim/Valkurm Slivers, via Hieroglyphics at Tavnazian Safehold (H-9); 15 min up to 2h. Horizon-marked: repeatable "Twice per conquest tally"; Horizon-specific "Dream Gear" (Sack of Dream Sand, Dream Ribbon, Dream Collar).
- Net: the wiki places Mirage pieces in Dynamis (Beaucedine body, Xarcabard head, Tavnazia for both per the item pages) but does not say which Dynamis mob drops them or record any Horizon-specific drop change. The zone pages are internally inconsistent (Tavnazia omits BLU).
