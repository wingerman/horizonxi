# BLU 75 magical / breath / drain / enfeeble / cure gear on HorizonXI

Researched 2026-11-07 against https://horizonffxi.wiki (the only stats source used). Every stat below is quoted from the item's Horizon wiki page as fetched; items whose page returned 404 are marked "no wiki page" with no stats. Retail wikis were used only to brainstorm candidate names.

Era constraints applied: CoP endgame + ToAU (released 2026-08-07) minus Out-of-Era content (Assault, Nyzul, Salvage, ISNM, ZNM, Besieged, Imperial Standing). Mirage (relic) treated as unavailable per the brief; Magus +1 (AF+1 via Sagheera/Limbus) IS available.

Legend for "Horizon-available?": **yes** = page exists, era-legal source; **no** = page exists but source is out-of-era or job list excludes BLU; **unsure** = page exists but acquisition is ambiguous on Horizon.

---

## 0. Key findings up front

- **Rostrum Pumps are NOT BLU on Horizon** (jobs line: "Lv. 75 WHM / BLM / BRD / SMN / PUP / SCH / GEO"). Drop them from the profile.
- **Zenith Mitts are NOT BLU** ("Lv. 73 WHM / BLM / RDM / BRD / SMN / GEO"). **Kirin's Osode is NOT BLU** on Horizon's page (WAR, MNK, BST, BRD, RNG, SAM, NIN). **Genie Weskit / Igqira Tiara / Demon Helm +1 are BLM(-ish) only.**
- **Sorcerer's Ring** page lists "Lv. 50 BLM" as the job line. On retail it is all-jobs; Horizon's page says BLM. Treat as unavailable to BLU until confirmed in-game.
- **Novio Earring (MAB +7) is in era on Horizon**: it comes from Jailer of Love (Sea), not ZNM. Best MAB ear.
- **Static Earring** (magic burst bonus) is an Apocalypse Nigh reward (CoP final quest) -> available. The only magic-burst piece found.
- **Tamas Ring is Horizon-customized**: now "Magic Accuracy +5" and "Enmity -5" (retail had neither Macc nor -5). Excellent macc/INT/MND ring for BLU.
- **Immortal's Scimitar is Horizon-customized**: "Magic Accuracy +5" and "Magic Atk. Bonus +4" added (BLU AF weapon, Lv 40) — a real option for the main-hand when not using an elemental staff.
- **Magus Charuqs (+1)** customized: "Magic Def. Bonus" +1 (+2 on +1). **Magus Shalwar +1** customized: Haste +2% added.
- **Walahra Turban is Horizon-customized DOWN** to "HP +5 MP +5 Haste +1%" (from HP/MP+30, Haste 5%) and requires 1,000 Imperial Bronze Pieces (bought with Imperial Standing). Not worth chasing even if IS were obtainable.
- **Homam Cosciales carry "Enhances Fast Cast effect"** (plus Haste +3%) and Homam Zucchetto carries "Magic Accuracy +4" — Homam (Limbus Omega) is the best BLU-equippable precast/haste set in era.
- **Nashira set (Limbus Ultima) is BLU-equippable** on Horizon (WHM/BLM/RDM/SMN/BLU): Magic Accuracy +5 (turban, manteel), +3 (gages, seraweels), +2 (crackows), plus Haste.
- Pages with **no wiki page (404)**: Lemegeton Medallion, Omega Ring, Witch Sash, Ixion Cape.
- **Elemental staves**: all 16 (8 NQ, 8 HQ) exist, "All Jobs", Lv 51, Woodworking 75 synthesis only (HQ = HQ1 of NQ recipe). **Obis**: all 8 exist, All Jobs Lv 71, quest "In the Name of Science" (Sea organs + chip + Silver Obi); not tagged Out of Era.
- **No "Breath damage +" item** exists for BLU in era except Mirage Keffiyeh (relic, unavailable per brief). Happy Egg (HP +1%) exists on Horizon via the Egg-stravaganza event.
