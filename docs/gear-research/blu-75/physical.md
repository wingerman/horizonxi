# BLU 75 physical gear research — HorizonXI (researched 2026-11-07)

Scope: physical blue magic spells + sword weaponskills, for a LuAshitacast profile.
Source rule: every stat below is quoted from the item's page on https://horizonffxi.wiki/ (fetched via WebFetch). No retail-wiki numbers are used. "W:" = wiki URL.

Mechanics assumed (from the caller's brief, not re-verified here): physical spell damage = D(Blue Magic skill) + fSTR + WSC; gear Attack does not affect spells; hit rate uses main-hand accuracy with no first-hit bonus; TP scales fTP spells; crit-modifier spells benefit from crit rate / DEX.

Availability legend: **yes** = in-era content with a Horizon source on the page; **no** = source is Assault / Nyzul / ISNM / Imperial Standing / Mirage, or job list excludes BLU; **unsure** = page exists but source ambiguous.

Pages that did not load (404 or empty): Mavi Scarf, Cuchulain's Mantle, Ecphoria Ring, Antares Harness, Kemas Earring, Blue_Mage_Primer (at that URL), Category:Store_TP, Velocious Belt (page body empty/protected). No stats are listed for these.

### Magus Jubbah vs Scorpion Harness +1 (and Homam Corazza)
- Magus Jubbah (+1) is the only body that adds *damage* to physical spells: Blue Magic Skill +15 raises base D, and STR +3/+5 raises fSTR. Scorpion Harness +1 adds nothing to damage, only Accuracy +12.
- For pure accuracy, **Homam Corazza (Accuracy +15)** beats SH+1 (Accuracy +12) and is BLU-usable, so SH+1 is only the budget option.
- Rule for the profile: default body = **Magus Jubbah +1** (or Jubbah). Swap to Homam Corazza only in the accuracy overlay for multi-hit spells (Disseverment, Hysteric Barrage, Quadratic Continuum) against evasive targets, where each lost hit costs a whole hit's worth of D. When hit rate is near cap, Jubbah wins outright. The exact crossover (how far below cap +12/+15 acc beats +15 skill +5 STR) depends on target level and is not something the wiki states; treat it as a tuning knob, not a fact.
