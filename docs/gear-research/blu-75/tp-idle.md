# BLU75 on HorizonXI: TP / idle / resting / utility gear (verified against horizonffxi.wiki)

Research date: 2026-11-07. Every stat below was read from the item's page on https://horizonffxi.wiki via WebFetch. "No wiki page" means the URL returned HTTP 404 (tried both `'` and `%27` spellings where relevant). Retail knowledge was only used to pick candidate names.

Horizon context that reshaped the lists:

- **Suppanomimi lost Dual Wield on Horizon.** Its "Enhances Dual Wield" was moved to **Stealth Earring** (Lv70, Sagheera, 75 Ancient Beastcoins). Suppa is now DEX+2 / Evasion skill+5 / Dagger skill+5 / Katana skill+5.
- **Walahra Turban is nerfed to Haste +1% / HP+5 / MP+5** (retail 5% / 30 / 30). Haste value is flagged "verification needed". Still All Jobs Lv75, 1,000 Imperial Bronze Pieces to Gajaad in Al Zahbi.
- **Speed Belt is Haste +5%** (retail 6%), Lv55, BLU can wear. Drops from King Arthro and from Horizon-added KSNM rewards (Horns of War, The Hills Are Alive, Early Bird Catches the Wyrm).
- **Joyeuse is not BLU on Horizon** (jobs line: WAR / RDM / PLD / BRD / DRG / COR / DNC). Justice Sword is RDM/THF/PLD. Ridill is WAR/THF/DRK/BST/RNG/SAM. Hauteclaire is PLD/DRK/RUN. None of the famous multi-hit swords are usable.
- **Magus Shalwar +1 has Horizon-added Haste +2%.** Magus Charuqs have Horizon-added Magic Def. Bonus +1; Charuqs +1 have MDB +2. The +1 path exists (trade AF + Luminian Thread + Flameshun Cloth + Wamoura Cloth / Khromated Leather + Ancient Beastcoins to Sagheera).
- **Peacock Amulet** comes from BCNM "Under Observation" (Horlais Peak, Lv40), not Argus; **Peacock Charm** (non-Ex, auctionable) comes from Argus. Same stats on both.
- **Bounding Boots** (Ex) from BCNM "Petrifying Pair"; **Empress Hairpin** (Ex) from BCNM "Dropping Like Flies". Non-Ex versions still drop from Leaping Lizzy / Valkurm Emperor.
- **Sattva Ring** is Horizon-changed: Damage taken -5% added, Enmity +5 (relevant to the HP/PDT researcher too).
- **Checkered Scarf** Horizon-changed: HP -12 (not +12), INT +2 added, hMP+1 / hHP+1.
- **Immortal's Scimitar** Horizon-changed: Magic Accuracy +5 and Magic Atk. Bonus +4 added.
- **Ducal Aketon** is a Horizon custom item (quest "Omni Aketon", Rank 10 in all three nations, trade all three nation Aketons to Milia in Ru'Lude Gardens): Movement speed +12% in the three nations, DEF 1, crystal bonus, Lv1 All Jobs.
- **Warp Ring** exists but is Hardcore-mode only.
- Out of era and therefore unavailable even though pages exist: Potent Belt and Chivalrous Chain (Assault points), Wightslayer and Velocious Belt (Nyzul), Pahluwan Khazagand (Assault), Organics (ZNM). Mirage Keffiyeh has a page (Dynamis - Xarcabard / Tavnazia) but per the brief Mirage does not drop on Horizon; treat as unavailable.
- Blue Cotehardie is Horizon-changed (1 tic of refresh under 41 MP) but its jobs line is RDM / THF / RNG / NIN / COR / DNC / RUN, so BLU cannot wear it here.
